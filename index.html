Yuqian Zheng (郑予谦)

I’m an undergraduate at Fudan University majoring in computer science. Since July 2026, I’ve been a research intern at the Fudan Vision and Learning Laboratory (FVL), co-advised by Prof. Xiaosong Jia and Prof. Junchi Yan. From July to August 2026, I was also a research intern at Fudan’s CodeWisdom Lab under the supervision of Prof. Xin Peng. Since December 2025, I’ve contributed to the research and development of WisePen, an AI agent platform for university teaching and research.

My research interests are in embodied AI and agentic robotics, with a primary focus on vision-language-action (VLA) models. I’m also interested in AI for education and in finance. I’m not currently working on world models. If any of these interests overlap with yours, feel free to reach out. I’d be happy to chat.

I’m from Quzhou, Zhejiang, and graduated from Quzhou No. 2 High School. I used to compete in informatics, earning gold at the ICPC Asia Hangzhou Regional as an invited contestant, silver at the NOI Winter Camp, and 28th place in Zhejiang at NOIP 2023. I no longer compete. Outside of research, I enjoy the humanities and hiking. I’d love to travel the world, see how people live in different places, and pay attention to the everyday lives that rarely make the headlines.
